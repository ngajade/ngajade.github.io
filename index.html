my WORKFLOW

AI supports my process without replacing design judgment. I stay focused on understanding the problem, making decisions and validating the solution.

Steps: 04
Step 01

Discover

Review requirements, clarify business logic, user roles and edge cases. I use the current product to understand how people work and where the experience can improve.

AIConnects to project documents through MCP to summarize and analyse requirements.

Step 02

Design

Turn requirements into user flows and interactive prototypes. Quick HTML demos help validate the flow before refining the interface in Figma.

AISpeeds up working prototypes so the team can experience and review the solution directly.

Step 03

Review & handoff

Review clickable prototypes, incorporate feedback and prepare clear Figma specifications and interaction notes for accurate development.

Step 04

Staging QA

Check layout, interaction, responsive behaviour and edge cases on the implemented product, then consolidate feedback for the next iteration.

AI accelerates the process, but it does not make the product decisions.

It helps me read, prototype and explore faster. Decisions about flow, structure, usability and what needs to be challenged remain my responsibility.
