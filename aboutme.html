ABOUT ME

UX/UI
Designer

With 4 years of experience in product-based environments, I have contributed to end-to-end product development—from business analysis and user-flow design to UI implementation, testing, and UX improvements.

With a mindset of continuous improvement and a commitment to putting care into every design, I believe I can bring practical value to every business I work with.

I'm Nga Pham

I was born and raised in Ho Chi Minh City and hold a bachelor’s degree in International Business Administration. After graduating and working for a year, I changed careers to pursue my long-standing passion for design. I completed the DIM program at Arena Multimedia and now have 4 years of experience designing digital products in a product-based environment.
Starting my design career later than some of my peers has motivated me to keep learning, improving, and making the most of every opportunity. Above all, I am deeply grateful to my family for supporting me throughout this journey.
